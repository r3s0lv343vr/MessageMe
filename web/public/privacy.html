Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

MessageMe is an Android app for sending letters to yourself. This page describes what we collect and where it lives. It is written in plain language for Google Play and for people who use the app.

Who we are

MessageMe is published by the developer of the Google Play listing for application ID com.unbound.messageme. Contact that developer through the Play Console / Play Store listing, or the email you used when you signed up.

What the Android app stores on your phone

On first use you must enter your first name, last name, and a valid email. There is no password. Those details stay in the app’s storage on that device so MessageMe knows who the letters are from.

The notes you write, their times, and related settings (for example notification preference and envelope hour) are also stored on your phone. They are not uploaded to our servers. The app is built to work offline.

If you allow notifications, Android may show your letter on the lock screen and in the notification shade. You can turn delivery off in Settings.

Settings also lets you export a backup file (JSON, CSV, or PDF) to wherever you save it. That file is yours. We do not receive it unless you choose to send it to someone.

What the website collects

If you submit first name, last name, and email on message-me-dusky.vercel.app, that form is sent to the site operator so they can follow up. We do not sell that list.

Google Play testing

If you join an internal or closed test, Google Play may share your tester email and some usage information with the developer, as Google’s tester page explains. That is Google’s process, not extra tracking we added inside the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not require a password.
  • We do not show ads in the app today.
  • We do not sell your letters or your email.
  • We do not use your letters to train public AI models.

How to delete your data

On the phone: uninstall MessageMe, or Android Settings → Apps → MessageMe → Storage → Clear storage. That removes the local name, email, and letters on that device.

If you sent your name and email through the website form, email the developer and ask for that signup to be deleted.

Children

MessageMe is not directed at children under 13. Do not use it to create an account for a child if that is not allowed where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
